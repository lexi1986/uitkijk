Printed in the service log, e.g. with docker compose logs camsvc. This way not everyone on your network can do the setup.

1. Cameras

Choose the brand and enter the IP address and login of the camera. Test makes the service fetch an image right away.

2. Home Assistant (optional)

Via MQTT, Home Assistant gets sensors, snapshots and notifications. If you use the Mosquitto app in HA, enter the address of HA and an HA user. Leaving this empty is fine too.
